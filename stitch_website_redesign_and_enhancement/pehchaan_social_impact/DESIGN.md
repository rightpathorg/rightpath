## Brand & Style

This design system expresses compassionate credibility, community warmth, and grassroots efficacy. Tailored for non-governmental organizations dedicated to societal upliftment, education, and health equity, the visual language balances the solemnity of institutional stewardship with the vitality of human-centric progress.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Organic Minimalism with Tactile Framing**. Rather than relying on synthetic drop shadows or cold digital abstractions, the UI employs warm, botanical earth tones, delicate structural borders, and stacked surface-container levels. The emotional response is welcoming, transparent, and direct—cultivating donor trust while honoring community narratives through thoughtful bilingual harmony.

## Colors

The palette draws deeply from verdant nature and organic pigments:

- **Primary (`#176b48`)**: A grounded emerald green representing growth, stability, and integrity. Used for primary calls-to-action, key badges, progress meters, and dominant brand anchors.
- **Secondary (`#153f30`)**: Deep pine forest. Establishes institutional foundation across high-contrast hero regions, dark feature panels, and prominent typography.
- **Tertiary (`#55b77e`)**: Vibrant leaf green. Deployed selectively as an energetic accent for eyebrows, tags, and progress highlights against dark backgrounds.
- **Neutral (`#24332b`)**: Deep forest charcoal. Replaces harsh synthetic blacks for all primary body text, offering high contrast with a warm, organic undertone.

### Extended Tokens & Roles
- **`surface-pale` (`#f6f8f5`)**: Default alternative canvas tint for sectional rhythm, offsetting crisp white card surfaces.
- **`surface-mint` (`#eaf4eb`)**: Soft highlight backing for illustration apertures, image frames, and metric callouts.
- **`border-subtle` (`#e6eee8`)**: Micro-borders applied around surfaces to define spatial cards without heavy elevation.
- **`text-muted` (`#64746b`)**: Balanced sage-slate for auxiliary labels, metadata, captions, and secondary body details.

## Typography

The typography couples geometric legibility with warm, approachable open counters using **Plus Jakarta Sans**. It pairs seamlessly with modern Devanagari web fonts for bilingual English and Hindi collateral, maintaining structural balance and vertical alignment across multi-script layouts.

### Usage Guidelines
- **Hero & Display (`display`, `display-mobile`)**: Apply strictly to primary mission statements and impact headers. Always preserve tight negative letter-spacing for refined editorial polish.
- **Section Titles (`headline-lg`, `headline-md`)**: Direct narrative milestones. Use the mobile variations below 640px to ensure accessibility and prevent unwanted text wrapping.
- **Eyebrows & Micro-Badges (`label-sm`)**: Uppercase styling with expanded tracking (`0.08em`) to delineate operational categories, tags, and campaign kickers.
- **Bilingual Harmony**: When rendering Devanagari text (e.g., *पहचान*), ensure font sizes match Latin baselines and line-heights are raised by a minimum factor of `0.1` to prevent accent clipping.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses a fluid-responsive 12-column grid constrained to a maximum content width of `1120px` (`70rem`).

### Rhythm & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px)**: 4 columns with `gutter: 1rem` and outer page `margin: 1rem`. Component sections feature `2.5rem` vertical spacing.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px)**: 8 columns with `gutter: 1rem` and outer `margin: 1.5rem`. Section padding expands to `3.5rem`.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12 columns with `gutter-desktop: 1.5rem` and outer `margin-desktop: 2rem`. Section vertical padding expands to `space-2xl` (`4rem`) to `5rem` for immersive impact.

### Spatial Discipline
- **Information Density**: Impact statistics and pillar blocks use fixed-gap distribution (`space-sm` to `space-md`) on mobile, flowing into equal-width multi-column matrices on desktop.
- **Text Wrapping & Bounds**: Editorial long-form copy must never exceed `45rem` (approx. 720px) to sustain optimal optical reading length.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally departs from floating skeuomorphism, utilizing **Tonal Layers and Micro-Borders** to establish spatial clarity.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Base Surface**: Warm off-white (`#ffffff`) or pale moss (`surface-pale`).
2. **Structural Enclosures (Cards, Modules)**: Elevated purely through edge contrast via `1px solid #e6eee8` against canvas backgrounds.
3. **Hero & Dark Modals**: Deep forest green surfaces (`#153f30`) hosting semi-translucent glass cards (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `0.14`) with faint boundary rings (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).
4. **Header Navigation**: Floating sticky positioning using an ambient, whisper-light veil shadow: `0 2px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern geometry (`roundedness: 2`). Standard modules and actionable containers use `0.5rem` (8px) to `0.75rem` (12px) radiuses, communicating modern craft while retaining structure.

- **Primary Cards & Containers**: Fixed at `0.625rem` (10px) to preserve crisp grid alignment.
- **Actions & Controls**: Buttons and text inputs use `0.375rem` (6px) to `0.5rem` (8px) for structured finger-target affordance.
- **Avatars, Ring Meters & Status Badges**: Full circle (`50%` or `9999px`) used for donor progress halos, status kickers, and community member portraits.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#176b48` fill with crisp `#ffffff` text, `0.5rem` corner radius, `space-sm` vertical by `space-lg` horizontal padding. Transitions to `#153f30` on hover.
- **Secondary Action (Outlined)**: Transparent background with `1px solid #176b48` border and `#176b48` text. When positioned over dark forest surfaces, transitions to `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` with white typography.
- **Icon Accents**: Inline donor symbols (hearts, seeds, hands) sit aligned with text, padded by `0.375rem`.

### Cards & Content Modules
- **Standard Card**: White (`#ffffff`) background, `1px solid #e6eee8` micro-border, `space-lg` inner padding, `0.625rem` rounded corners.
- **Project Card**: Dual-tier anatomy. Top aperture is a dedicated illustration/image container tinted with `surface-mint` (`#eaf4eb`), sitting directly flush against a bottom white description container bordered by `1px solid #e6eee8`.
- **Translucent Story Card**: Used exclusively on dark canvases. Composed of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.09)` background, micro-border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, and high-tint pale text (`#e1eee5`).

### Chips & Eyebrow Tags
- Rendered in all-caps using `label-sm`.
- Contained with pill or soft-rounded corners (`0.25rem`), featuring subtle green tint surfaces (`#eaf4eb`) and deep green typography (`#176b48`).

### Impact Metrics (Data Display)
- Features a signature accent border: `2px solid #80b98e` on the left border, indented by `space-sm` padding.
- Metric values rendered in `stat-metric` bold typography with labels underneath in `label-md` using `text-muted`.

### Form Inputs & Selection Controls
- **Inputs**: Minimal white background framed by `1px solid #e6eee8`, activating to a focused `1.5px solid #176b48` border ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Emerald tinted active state (`#176b48`) with stark white check indicators.

### Community Donor Halo (Unique Pattern)
- Circular donor avatar rings framed in a dynamic 2px gradient boundary (`linear-gradient(135deg, #16824e, #b7d9a7)`), separated from the internal graphic or portrait by a 2px off-white buffer.
