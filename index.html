Our Core Initiatives & Action Pillars

A comprehensive lifecycle approach addressing holistic growth from formative childhood education to elderly compassionate healthcare.

Education

1. Shiksha Abhiyan (शिक्षा अभियान)

Daily foundational literacy, numeracy, and digital labs targeting first-generation street learners in slum clusters.

Target: 5,000 Kids 78% Achieved
Nutrition

2. Poshan Sahayata (भोजन सहायता)

Clean, balanced warm mid-day meals and vitamin supplements preventing child malnutrition and micro-deficiencies.

Target: 4,000 Meals 92% Achieved
Healthcare

3. Arogya Seva (स्वास्थ्य सेवा)

Fortnightly mobile health checks, routine immunization drives, and adolescent menstrual hygiene kit distribution.

Target: 24 Camps/Qtr 65% Achieved
Livelihoods

4. Kaushal Vikas (कौशल विकास)

Vocational tailoring, basic IT courses, and handicraft artisan training enabling mothers to earn financial independence.

Target: 300 Graduates 84% Achieved
Elder Care

5. Vriddha Care (बुजुर्गों की सेवा)

Emotional companionship, free chronic medicine supplies, and assistive mobility equipment for abandoned senior citizens.

Target: 250 Elders 70% Achieved
Environment

6. Harit Pahal (पर्यावरण व स्वच्छता)

Community tree plantations, localized waste segregation awareness, and water sanitation filters in dense bastis.

Target: 2,000 Saplings 90% Achieved

The Origin Story (शुरुआत की कहानी)

At just 21, witnessing young children collecting scrap under Delhi flyovers moved Ashish deeply. He started by teaching alphabets under open tree canopies with a notebook and blackboard. That humble beginning has now blossomed into RightPath NGO.

code

Tech-Driven Mindset (BCA Graduate)

Holding a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) degree, Ashish infuses modern tech into grassroots social work. From basic computer labs for slum kids to 100% transparent digital donor ledgers, every rupee and student record is trackable.

handshake

Mission-Driven Grassroots Leadership

Ashish leads with a clear philosophy: charity should never foster dependence. RightPath programs are designed to graduate families out of poverty through education, digital literacy, and self-sustaining vocational workshops.

diversity_3

Youth & Community Mobilization

Under Ashish’s leadership, over 180+ college scholars and grassroots educators spend their weekends on the ground—teaching, packing warm meals, and conducting medical checks with deep community trust.
